8 · How the scores are computed

  • privacy = 1 − (0.5 × worst risk + 0.5 × mean risk): the worst risk counts on purpose, because one open door is enough.
  • utility = mean of scenario task scores (required but unanswerable → 0)
  • composite = weighted harmonic mean (privacy weight 30, utility weight 25): high only when both are high, so FIN-S2 composite is 0 while its utility is 0.
